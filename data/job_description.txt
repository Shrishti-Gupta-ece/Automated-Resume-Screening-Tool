Job Title: Data Analyst
Department: Analytics & Business Intelligence
Experience: Minimum 2 years of experience

Job Overview:
We are looking for an analytical and detail-oriented Data Analyst to join our team. In this role, you will analyze large datasets, design actionable reports, and help stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

Key Responsibilities:
- Extract, clean, and analyze complex datasets from relational databases.
- Develop, maintain, and automate recurring reports and dashboards.
- Collaborate with business teams to translate requirements into analytical insights.
- Perform exploratory data analysis and communicate findings effectively to stakeholders.

Required Skills:
- Python: Proficient in writing scripts for data extraction, manipulation, and analysis.
- SQL: Strong experience writing complex queries, joins, aggregations, and CTEs.
- Excel: Advanced knowledge of pivot tables, formulas, modeling, and data analysis.
- Experience: Minimum 2 years of professional experience in data or business analysis.

Preferred / Plus Skills:
- Power BI: Experience creating interactive dashboards and visualizations is a plus.
- Pandas: Practical knowledge of the Pandas library for data cleaning and manipulation is a plus.
- Git: Experience with Git version control and collaborative workflows is a plus.
- Strong problem-solving, critical thinking, and written/verbal communication skills.
